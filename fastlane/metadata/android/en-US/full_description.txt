Tchat is the mobile app for TensorGrid's AI chat service at chat.tensorgrid.space. Sign in with your TensorGrid account to chat with leading AI models, generate images and use agents, with your conversations kept in sync with the web app.

Why use the app instead of the browser?

* Smoother and faster — a real native app, so scrolling, typing and watching responses stream all feel snappier.
* Built for touch — swipe gestures, comfortable tap targets, and a layout that adapts to tablets and foldables.
* Integrated with your phone — share text, images and files into Tchat, pin a generated artifact to your home screen, get shortcuts to the models you use most, and save or share images through the system menus.
* Works offline — your chat list and any conversation you have opened are stored on the device, so you can keep reading with poor or no signal.

Features

* Chat — real-time streaming, message branching, stop/regenerate/fork, markdown with syntax highlighting, LaTeX maths, copyable code blocks, file attachments, tool-call progress cards, and an approval panel that asks before a tool runs.
* Models — a searchable model picker, starred models first, and a comparison mode that runs two models on the same prompt.
* Image generation — create images from a prompt and save or share them.
* Artifacts — Mermaid, SVG, HTML, React and Markdown artifacts open in a viewer, and any artifact can be pinned to the home screen.
* Agents — browse and use agents, plus MCP server configuration.
* Conversations — date-grouped list with tags, search, rename, archive, delete, share, duplicate and export.
* Voice — dictation and text-to-speech playback.
* Themes — light/dark modes and a choice of the Material or Liquid Glass interface style.
* Feedback — rate any reply, and flag offensive or harmful answers straight from the conversation.

Privacy

Your chats are sent to TensorGrid's servers to generate replies. The app contains no advertising and no analytics. If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report (device model, OS version and the error, with no messages, account details or IP address) is sent to TensorGrid's own crash-reporting server so we can fix it. Dictation may use your phone's speech-recognition service. Privacy policy: https://tensorgrid.space/privacy

Compatibility

Built against LibreChat v0.8.8, the open-source platform Tchat's service runs on. Tchat is based on the open-source Switchboard client and is not affiliated with the LibreChat project.
